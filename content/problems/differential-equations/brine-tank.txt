topic: differential-equations/basics
title: Salt in a brine tank
answer: A
hint: The volume stays at 500 L because water flows in and out at the same rate.

A tank holds 500 L of pure water. Brine with 0.05 kg of salt per litre flows in at 10 L/min, and the well-mixed solution flows out at 10 L/min. How much salt is in the tank after 30 minutes?

A. 11.28 kg    B. 13.72 kg    C. 15.00 kg    D. 25.00 kg

## Solution
Salt in: $0.05 \times 10 = 0.5$ kg/min. Salt out: $\dfrac{Q}{500} \times 10 = \dfrac{Q}{50}$ kg/min.
---
$\dfrac{dQ}{dt} = 0.5 - \dfrac{Q}{50}$ with $Q(0) = 0$ gives $Q = 25\left(1 - e^{-t/50}\right)$.
---
$Q(30) = 25\left(1 - e^{-0.6}\right) = 11.28$ kg.
