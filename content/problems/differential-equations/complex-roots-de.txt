title: Equation from a damped solution
answer: D
hint: Read the roots of the characteristic equation off the exponential and the cosine.

Which differential equation has the general solution $y = e^{-x}(C_1\cos 2x + C_2\sin 2x)$?

A. $y'' - 2y' + 5y = 0$    B. $y'' + 2y' + 3y = 0$    C. $y'' + y' + 4y = 0$    D. $y'' + 2y' + 5y = 0$

## Solution
The roots are $r = -1 \pm 2i$.
---
Sum of roots $= -2$, product $= (-1)^2 + 2^2 = 5$.
---
$r^2 + 2r + 5 = 0$, so the DE is $y'' + 2y' + 5y = 0$.
