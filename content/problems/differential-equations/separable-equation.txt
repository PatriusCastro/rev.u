topic: differential-equations/basics
title: A separable equation
answer: D
hint: Divide both sides by $y$ before integrating.

Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2y$ with $y(0) = 2$. What is $y(1)$?

A. 3.000    B. 40.171    C. 7.389    D. 5.437

## Solution
Separate: $\dfrac{dy}{y} = 3x^2\,dx$.
---
Integrate: $\ln y = x^3 + C$, so $y = Ce^{x^3}$.
---
$y(0) = 2$ gives $C = 2$, so $y = 2e^{x^3}$.
---
$y(1) = 2e = 5.437$.
