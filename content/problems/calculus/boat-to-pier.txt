topic: calculus/applications
title: Banca pulled to the pier
answer: A
hint: The rope, the pier height and the boat's distance form a right triangle. Differentiate it before putting in numbers.

A banca is pulled toward a pier by a rope tied to its bow. The rope runs through a ring on the pier 4 m above the bow, and a man pulls the rope in at 1.2 m/s. How fast is the banca moving toward the pier when 5 m of rope is out?

A. 2.00 m/s    B. 1.20 m/s    C. 0.72 m/s    D. 1.60 m/s

## Solution
Let $x$ be the horizontal distance to the pier and $L$ the rope length: $x^2 + 4^2 = L^2$.
---
Differentiate: $2x\dfrac{dx}{dt} = 2L\dfrac{dL}{dt}$, so $\dfrac{dx}{dt} = \dfrac{L}{x}\dfrac{dL}{dt}$.
---
When $L = 5$, $x = \sqrt{25 - 16} = 3$ m.
---
$\dfrac{dx}{dt} = \dfrac{5}{3}(-1.2) = -2.00$ m/s. The banca approaches at 2.00 m/s.
