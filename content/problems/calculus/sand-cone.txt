topic: calculus/applications
title: Rising pile of sand
answer: C
hint: Use the fact that height equals radius to write the cone's volume in terms of height only.

A conveyor at a quarry in Rizal drops sand at 0.6 m³/min. The sand forms a cone whose height is always equal to its base radius. How fast is the height rising when the pile is 2 m high?

A. 0.150 m/min    B. 0.0159 m/min    C. 0.0477 m/min    D. 0.0955 m/min

## Solution
With $r = h$: $V = \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \dfrac{\pi h^3}{3}$.
---
Differentiate: $\dfrac{dV}{dt} = \pi h^2 \dfrac{dh}{dt}$.
---
$\dfrac{dh}{dt} = \dfrac{0.6}{\pi (2)^2} = 0.0477$ m/min.
