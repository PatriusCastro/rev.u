topic: algebra/word-problems
title: Seats in a barangay gym
answer: D
hint: Find the number of seats in the last row first. The 25th row has only 24 increases.

The bleachers of a barangay gym have 25 rows. The first row has 20 seats and each row after it has 3 more seats than the row in front. How many seats are there in all?

A. 1,475    B. 2,300    C. 1,150    D. 1,400

## Solution
Last row: $a_{25} = 20 + (25 - 1)(3) = 92$ seats.
---
$S_{25} = \dfrac{25}{2}(20 + 92) = 12.5 \times 112$.
---
$S_{25} = 1{,}400$ seats.
