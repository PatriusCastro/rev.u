topic: algebra/word-problems
title: Number with reversed digits
answer: B
hint: Write the number as 10t + u and its reverse as 10u + t.

The digits of a two-digit number add up to 11. When the digits are reversed, the new number is 27 more than the original. What is the original number?

A. 74    B. 47    C. 38    D. 56

## Solution
Let the number be $10t + u$, with $t + u = 11$.
---
$(10u + t) - (10t + u) = 27$, so $9(u - t) = 27$ and $u - t = 3$.
---
Adding to $t + u = 11$: $u = 7$, $t = 4$. The number is 47.
