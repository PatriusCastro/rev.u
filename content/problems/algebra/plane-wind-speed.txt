topic: algebra/word-problems
title: Wind on a Manila to Davao flight
answer: D
hint: With the wind the ground speed is plane plus wind; against it, plane minus wind.

A plane flies 1,200 km from Manila to Davao with a steady tailwind in 2 hours. On the return trip, against the same wind, it takes 2.5 hours. What is the speed of the wind?

A. 540 km/h    B. 120 km/h    C. 30 km/h    D. 60 km/h

## Solution
With the wind: $p + w = \dfrac{1200}{2} = 600$ km/h.
---
Against the wind: $p - w = \dfrac{1200}{2.5} = 480$ km/h.
---
Subtract: $2w = 120$, so $w = 60$ km/h.
