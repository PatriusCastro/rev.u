topic: algebra/word-problems
title: Cubic from its roots
answer: C
hint: Each root r gives a factor (x − r). Clear the fraction by writing the factor for 1/2 as (2x − 1).

Which polynomial has leading coefficient 2 and roots $2$, $-3$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$?

A. $2x^3 - x^2 - 13x - 6$    B. $2x^3 + x^2 - 11x + 6$    C. $2x^3 + x^2 - 13x + 6$    D. $2x^3 + x^2 - 13x - 6$

## Solution
$P(x) = 2(x - 2)(x + 3)\left(x - \dfrac{1}{2}\right) = (x - 2)(x + 3)(2x - 1)$.
---
$(x - 2)(x + 3) = x^2 + x - 6$.
---
$(x^2 + x - 6)(2x - 1) = 2x^3 - x^2 + 2x^2 - x - 12x + 6 = 2x^3 + x^2 - 13x + 6$.
