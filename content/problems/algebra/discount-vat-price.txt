title: List price behind a receipt
answer: A
hint: The discount and the VAT each multiply the price. Undo them by dividing, in reverse order.

A hardware store gives a 10% discount on the list price of a power drill, then adds 12% VAT to the discounted price. The customer pays ₱2,419.20. What is the list price?

A. ₱2,400.00    B. ₱2,371.76    C. ₱2,160.00    D. ₱2,341.79

## Solution
Amount paid $= 1.12 \times 0.90 \times P = 1.008P$.
---
$P = \dfrac{2419.20}{1.008}$.
---
$P = ₱2{,}400.00$.
