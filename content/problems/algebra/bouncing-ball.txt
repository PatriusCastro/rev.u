topic: algebra/word-problems
title: Total path of a bouncing ball
answer: A
hint: The first drop happens once. Every rebound goes up and comes back down.

A ball is dropped from a height of 12 m. Each time it hits the floor it rebounds to $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the height it fell from. What total distance does it travel before coming to rest?

A. 48.00 m    B. 30.00 m    C. 60.00 m    D. 36.00 m

## Solution
The rebound heights form an infinite geometric series: first term $12 \times \dfrac{3}{5} = 7.2$ m, ratio $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
---
Sum of rebound heights: $\dfrac{7.2}{1 - 0.6} = 18$ m. Each is travelled up and down: $2 \times 18 = 36$ m.
---
Total: $12 + 36 = 48.00$ m.
