topic: hydraulics/hydrostatics
title: Force on an irrigation sluice gate
answer: A
hint: Use the pressure at the centroid of the gate, not at its top or bottom edge.

A vertical rectangular sluice gate in an irrigation canal in Nueva Ecija is 1.5 m wide and 2.0 m high. Its top edge is 1.2 m below the water surface, with water on one side only. Find the total water force on the gate. Use $\gamma_w = 9.81$ kN/m³.

A. 64.75 kN    B. 94.18 kN    C. 35.32 kN    D. 43.16 kN

## Solution
Depth of the centroid: $\bar{h} = 1.2 + 1.0 = 2.2$ m.
---
Area: $1.5 \times 2.0 = 3.0$ m².
---
$F = 9.81(2.2)(3.0) = 64.75$ kN.
