topic: hydraulics/hydrodynamics
title: Jet on a single moving plate
answer: D
hint: A single plate moving away catches water only at the relative speed.

A horizontal water jet 50 mm in diameter leaves a nozzle at 30 m/s. It hits a single flat plate held at right angles to the jet. The plate moves away from the nozzle, along the jet, at a steady 12 m/s. Find the force of the jet on the plate. Neglect friction.

A. 1767 N    B. 1060 N    C. 424 N    D. 636 N

## Solution
Jet area: $A = \dfrac{\pi(0.05)^2}{4} = 0.001963$ m².
---
Relative speed: $v - u = 30 - 12 = 18$ m/s. A single plate only receives water at $Q' = A(v - u)$.
---
$F = \rho A(v - u)^2 = 1000(0.001963)(18)^2 = 636$ N.
