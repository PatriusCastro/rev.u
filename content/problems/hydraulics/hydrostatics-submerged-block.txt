topic: hydraulics/hydrostatics
title: Lowering a concrete block into the sea
answer: D
hint: The cable carries the block's weight less the buoyant force. Use the seawater's unit weight.

A crane lowers a solid concrete block, 0.4 m by 0.5 m by 0.6 m, into the sea at a port in Subic. Concrete weighs 23.5 kN/m³ and seawater weighs 10.1 kN/m³. Find the cable tension when the block hangs fully submerged and at rest.

A. 1.64 kN    B. 4.03 kN    C. 2.82 kN    D. 1.61 kN

## Solution
Volume: $0.4 \times 0.5 \times 0.6 = 0.12$ m³.
---
Weight: $23.5(0.12) = 2.82$ kN. Buoyant force: $10.1(0.12) = 1.212$ kN.
---
$T = 2.82 - 1.212 = 1.61$ kN.
