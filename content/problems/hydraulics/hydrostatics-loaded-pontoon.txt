topic: hydraulics/hydrostatics
title: Draft of a loaded timber pontoon
answer: B
hint: The displaced water must carry both the timber and the load.

A solid timber pontoon is 3 m long, 2 m wide and 0.8 m deep, and the timber has specific gravity 0.65. It floats level in fresh water on Laguna de Bay while carrying a 6 kN load placed at its centre. Find the depth of the pontoon below the water surface. Use $\gamma_w = 9.81$ kN/m³.

A. 0.52 m    B. 0.62 m    C. 0.18 m    D. 0.10 m

## Solution
Weight of timber: $0.65(9.81)(3 \times 2 \times 0.8) = 30.61$ kN, so the total weight is $36.61$ kN.
---
Buoyancy equals weight: $9.81(3 \times 2)D = 36.61$.
---
$D = 0.62$ m.
