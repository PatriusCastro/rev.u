topic: trigonometry/angles-of-elevation
answer: C
hint: The angle of depression from the top equals the angle of elevation from the boat.

From the top of a lighthouse 50 m above sea level, the angle of depression of a boat is 20°. How far is the boat from the foot of the lighthouse?

A. 18.20 m    B. 53.21 m    C. 137.37 m    D. 146.19 m

## Solution
The angle of elevation from the boat to the top is also 20°, because the two horizontals are parallel.
---
The height 50 m is opposite 20° and the distance $d$ is adjacent, so $\tan 20° = 50 / d$.
---
$d = 50 / \tan 20° = 50 / 0.3640 \approx 137.37$ m.
