topic: trigonometry/sine-cosine-law
title: Tree across a river
answer: D
hint: Find the third angle first. Then pair each side with the angle across from it.

Two surveyors stand at points A and B, 100 m apart along a straight riverbank. A tree C stands on the far bank. Angle CAB is 58° and angle CBA is 72°. How far is the tree from A?

A. 110.70 m    B. 80.55 m    C. 130.47 m    D. 124.15 m

## Solution
Angle $C = 180° - 58° - 72° = 50°$. Side AB = 100 m is opposite C.
---
AC is opposite angle B, so by the sine law $\dfrac{AC}{\sin 72°} = \dfrac{100}{\sin 50°}$.
---
$AC = \dfrac{100(0.9511)}{0.7660} = 124.15$ m.
