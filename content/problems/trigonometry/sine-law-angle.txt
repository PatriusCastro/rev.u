title: Angle by the sine law
answer: A
hint: You know angle A and the side across from it, plus side b. That pair is what the sine law needs.

In triangle ABC, $A = 50°$, $a = 15$ cm and $b = 10$ cm. Find angle B.

A. 30.71°    B. 59.29°    C. 149.29°    D. 33.33°

## Solution
Angle A and its opposite side are known, so use the sine law: $\dfrac{\sin B}{b} = \dfrac{\sin A}{a}$.
---
$\sin B = \dfrac{10 \sin 50°}{15} = \dfrac{10(0.7660)}{15} = 0.5107$, so $B = 30.71°$.
---
Check the obtuse option: $180° - 30.71° = 149.29°$ would make $A + B$ over 180°. So $B = 30.71°$ is the only answer.
