topic: trigonometry/angles-of-elevation
answer: A
hint: The shadow is the side next to the sun's angle, and the pole is the side across from it.

A vertical flagpole casts a shadow 12 m long on level ground when the angle of elevation of the sun is 52°. How tall is the flagpole?

A. 15.36 m    B. 9.46 m    C. 7.39 m    D. 19.49 m

## Solution
The shadow (12 m) is adjacent to the 52° angle and the height $h$ is opposite it.
---
$\tan 52° = h / 12$, so $h = 12 \tan 52°$.
---
$h = 12(1.2799) \approx 15.36$ m.
