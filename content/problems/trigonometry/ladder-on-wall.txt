topic: trigonometry/angles-of-elevation
answer: B
hint: The ladder is the hypotenuse. Which ratio links the hypotenuse to the side opposite 60°?

A ladder 6 m long leans against a vertical wall and makes an angle of 60° with the level ground. How high up the wall does the top of the ladder reach?

A. 3.00 m    B. 5.20 m    C. 6.93 m    D. 10.39 m

## Solution
The ladder is the hypotenuse, 6 m. The height $h$ on the wall is opposite the 60° angle.
---
$\sin 60° = h / 6$, so $h = 6 \sin 60°$.
---
$h = 6(0.8660) \approx 5.20$ m.
