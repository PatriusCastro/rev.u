From point A, the angle of elevation of the top of a tower is 30°. From point B, 40 m closer to the tower, it is 60°. Find the height of the tower.

A. 20.00 m    B. 28.28 m    C. 34.64 m    D. 40.00 m

## Solution
Let $x$ be the distance from B to the tower and $h$ its height.
---
From B: $\tan 60° = h / x$, so $h = \sqrt{3}\,x$.
---
From A: $\tan 30° = h / (x + 40)$, so $x + 40 = \sqrt{3}\,h = 3x$ and $x = 20$ m.
---
$h = 20\sqrt{3} \approx 34.64$ m.
