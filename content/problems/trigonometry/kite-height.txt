topic: trigonometry/angles-of-elevation
title: Height of a kite
answer: B
hint: The string is the hypotenuse, and the height is across from the angle.

A kite is flying on a straight string 80 m long that makes an angle of 40° with the level ground. How high is the kite above the ground?

A. 61.28 m    B. 51.42 m    C. 67.13 m    D. 95.34 m

## Solution
The string is the hypotenuse (80 m) and the height $h$ is opposite the 40° angle, so $\sin 40° = \dfrac{h}{80}$.
---
$h = 80 \sin 40° = 80(0.6428) = 51.42$ m.
