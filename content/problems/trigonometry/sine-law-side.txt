topic: trigonometry/sine-cosine-law
answer: C
hint: You know side a and the angle opposite it. That pair unlocks the sine law.

In triangle ABC, $A = 40°$, $B = 60°$ and $a = 10$ cm. Find side $b$.

A. 7.42 cm    B. 11.70 cm    C. 13.47 cm    D. 15.32 cm

## Solution
Side $a$ and angle $A$ are a known opposite pair, so use the sine law: $\dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{a}{\sin A}$.
---
$b = \dfrac{10 \sin 60°}{\sin 40°} = \dfrac{10(0.8660)}{0.6428}$.
---
$b \approx 13.47$ cm.
