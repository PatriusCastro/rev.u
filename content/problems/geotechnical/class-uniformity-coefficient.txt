topic: geotechnical/classification
title: Uniformity of a river sand
answer: C
hint: The uniformity coefficient uses only two of the three sizes, and the effective size is one of them.

A sieve analysis of sand quarried from the Pampanga River gives $D_{10} = 0.12$ mm, $D_{30} = 0.45$ mm and $D_{60} = 1.30$ mm. Find the uniformity coefficient $C_u = D_{60}/D_{10}$.

A. 2.89    B. 1.30    C. 10.83    D. 3.75

## Solution
The effective size is $D_{10} = 0.12$ mm.
---
$C_u = \dfrac{D_{60}}{D_{10}} = \dfrac{1.30}{0.12} = 10.83$
