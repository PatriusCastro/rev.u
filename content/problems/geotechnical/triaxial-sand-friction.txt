title: Drained triaxial test on clean sand
answer: A
hint: The deviator stress is not the major principal stress. Add the cell pressure first.

A consolidated-drained triaxial test is run on a clean sand ($c = 0$). The cell pressure is 80 kPa and the deviator stress at failure is 200 kPa. Find the angle of internal friction.

A. 33.75°    B. 25.38°    C. 29.05°    D. 45.58°

## Solution
$\sigma_3 = 80$ kPa and $\sigma_1 = 80 + 200 = 280$ kPa.
---
$\sin\phi = \dfrac{\sigma_1 - \sigma_3}{\sigma_1 + \sigma_3} = \dfrac{200}{360} = 0.5556$.
---
$\phi = \sin^{-1}(0.5556) = 33.75°$.
