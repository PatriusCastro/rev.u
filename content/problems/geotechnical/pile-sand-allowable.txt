topic: geotechnical/slopes-piles
title: Allowable load on a pile in sand
answer: C
hint: Skin friction uses the average effective stress along the pile; end bearing uses the stress at the tip.

A 450 mm diameter bored pile is installed 12 m into a dry sand with $\gamma = 18$ kN/m³. The water table is well below the tip. Use $K = 1.2$ and $\delta = 24°$ for skin friction, $f_s = K\sigma'_v\tan\delta$ with $\sigma'_v$ averaged over the pile length, and end bearing $q'N_qA_p$ with $N_q = 55$, ignoring any limit on tip resistance or critical depth. With a factor of safety of 3, find the allowable load.

A. 326.3 kN    B. 1282.4 kN    C. 956.1 kN    D. 901.7 kN

## Solution
$p = \pi(0.45) = 1.4137$ m, $A_p = \dfrac{\pi(0.45)^2}{4} = 0.15904$ m².
---
Average $\sigma'_v = 18(6) = 108$ kPa. $f_s = 1.2(108)\tan 24° = 57.70$ kPa.
---
$Q_s = 57.70(1.4137)(12) = 978.9$ kN.
---
$q' = 18(12) = 216$ kPa. $Q_p = 216(55)(0.15904) = 1889.4$ kN.
---
$Q_a = \dfrac{978.9 + 1889.4}{3} = 956.1$ kN.
