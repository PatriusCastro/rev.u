topic: geotechnical/foundations
title: Stress below a footing by the 2:1 method
answer: B
hint: The loaded area grows by $z/2$ on each side, so each side length grows by $z$.

A 2.5 m by 2.5 m footing carries a total load of 900 kN. Using the 2:1 method, find the average vertical stress increase 3 m below the base of the footing.

A. 56.25 kPa    B. 29.75 kPa    C. 144.00 kPa    D. 12.46 kPa

## Solution
At $z = 3$ m each side becomes $2.5 + 3 = 5.5$ m.
---
$\Delta\sigma = \dfrac{900}{(5.5)(5.5)} = \dfrac{900}{30.25}$.
---
$\Delta\sigma = 29.75$ kPa.
