title: Coefficient of curvature of a gravelly sand
answer: A
hint: Only one of the three sizes is squared, and it goes on top.

A base course material for a road in Tarlac has $D_{10} = 0.20$ mm, $D_{30} = 0.62$ mm and $D_{60} = 1.40$ mm. Find the coefficient of curvature $C_c = \dfrac{D_{30}^2}{D_{60}D_{10}}$.

A. 1.37    B. 2.21    C. 0.73    D. 7.00

## Solution
$D_{30}^2 = 0.62^2 = 0.3844$ mm²
---
$D_{60}D_{10} = 1.40(0.20) = 0.28$ mm²
---
$C_c = \dfrac{0.3844}{0.28} = 1.37$
