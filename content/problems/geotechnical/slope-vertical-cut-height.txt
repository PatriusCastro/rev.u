title: Critical height of a vertical cut
answer: D
hint: The critical height depends only on cohesion and unit weight.

A contractor in Quezon City plans an unbraced vertical excavation in a stiff clay with $c = 28$ kPa, $\phi = 0$ and $\gamma = 17.8$ kN/m³. Using $H_{cr} = \dfrac{4c}{\gamma}$, find the critical height of the cut.

A. 3.15 m    B. 4.72 m    C. 4.19 m    D. 6.29 m

## Solution
$H_{cr} = \dfrac{4c}{\gamma} = \dfrac{4(28)}{17.8}$.
---
$H_{cr} = 6.29$ m.
