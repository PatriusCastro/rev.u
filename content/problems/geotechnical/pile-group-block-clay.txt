topic: geotechnical/slopes-piles
title: Pile group in clay, block check
answer: C
hint: Compute both the sum of single piles and the block, then keep the smaller.

A 3 × 3 group of 350 mm square piles at 1.0 m centre-to-centre spacing is driven 14 m into a uniform clay with $c_u = 50$ kPa. For a single pile use $Q_u = 9c_uA_p + \alpha c_u pL$ with $\alpha = 0.75$. For block failure use $Q_{block} = 9c_uB_gL_g + 2(B_g + L_g)c_uL$, where the block plan size is measured to the outer faces of the piles. Find the ultimate capacity of the group.

A. 9065.1 kN    B. 6580.0 kN    C. 7111.1 kN    D. 7420.1 kN

## Solution
Single pile: $9(50)(0.35^2) + 0.75(50)(4 \times 0.35)(14) = 55.1 + 735.0 = 790.1$ kN.
---
Nine piles: $9(790.125) = 7111.1$ kN.
---
Block: $B_g = L_g = 2(1.0) + 0.35 = 2.35$ m.
---
$Q_{block} = 9(50)(2.35)^2 + 2(2.35 + 2.35)(50)(14) = 2485.1 + 6580.0 = 9065.1$ kN.
---
The smaller governs: $Q_g = 7111.1$ kN (individual pile failure).
