topic: geotechnical/foundations
title: Ultimate bearing capacity of a wall footing
answer: B
hint: A strip footing has three terms. The overburden at the footing base counts too.

A wall footing 1.8 m wide rests 1.2 m below the ground surface. The soil has $\gamma = 18$ kN/m³, $c = 15$ kPa and $\phi = 28°$. The water table is deep. Using Terzaghi's equation with $N_c = 31.61$, $N_q = 17.81$ and $N_\gamma = 13.70$, find the ultimate bearing capacity.

A. 696.1 kPa    B. 1080.8 kPa    C. 1178.6 kPa    D. 1302.7 kPa

## Solution
$q = \gamma D_f = 18(1.2) = 21.6$ kPa.
---
$q_u = cN_c + qN_q + 0.5\gamma BN_\gamma = 15(31.61) + 21.6(17.81) + 0.5(18)(1.8)(13.70)$.
---
$q_u = 474.15 + 384.70 + 221.94 = 1080.8$ kPa.
