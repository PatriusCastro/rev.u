title: Garden plot on a circle
answer: B
hint: All four corners lie on one circle, so a Heron-like formula for four sides applies.

A quadrilateral garden plot has sides 5 m, 6 m, 7 m and 8 m in order, and all four corners lie on a circular walkway. What is the area of the plot?

A. 42.00 m²    B. 40.99 m²    C. 20.49 m²    D. 81.98 m²

## Solution
Half-perimeter: $s = \dfrac{5+6+7+8}{2} = 13$.
---
Brahmagupta: $A = \sqrt{(13-5)(13-6)(13-7)(13-8)} = \sqrt{8(7)(6)(5)} = \sqrt{1680}$.
---
$A = 40.99$ m².
