title: Glass tetrahedron paperweight
answer: B
hint: The top vertex sits above the centroid of the base triangle. Find that height, then use one third of base area times height.

A glass paperweight is a regular tetrahedron with every edge 6 cm. What is its volume?

A. 31.18 cm³    B. 25.46 cm³    C. 18.00 cm³    D. 76.37 cm³

## Solution
Base area: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}(6)^2 = 15.59$ cm². Height: $h = 6\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}} = 4.899$ cm.
---
$V = \tfrac{1}{3}(15.59)(4.899) = 25.46$ cm³.
---
Check: $V = \dfrac{a^3\sqrt{2}}{12} = \dfrac{216\sqrt{2}}{12} = 25.46$ cm³.
