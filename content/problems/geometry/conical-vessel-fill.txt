title: Filling a conical vessel
answer: A
hint: The water forms a cone similar to the vessel. How does volume scale with depth?

A conical vessel stands with its tip pointing down. When the water is 30 cm deep it holds 1.6 L. How much more water must be added to raise the depth to 45 cm?

A. 3.8 L    B. 5.4 L    C. 2.0 L    D. 0.8 L

## Solution
Depth ratio: $k = \dfrac{45}{30} = 1.5$. Volume scales with $k^3$.
---
Volume at 45 cm: $1.6(1.5)^3 = 1.6(3.375) = 5.4$ L.
---
Water to add: $5.4 - 1.6 = 3.8$ L.
