title: Three pipes touching
answer: C
hint: Each side of the triangle joins two centres. How far apart are the centres of two touching circles?

Three round pipes with radii 3 cm, 5 cm and 7 cm are laid side by side so that each pipe touches the other two from outside. What is the area of the triangle formed by joining their centres?

A. 6.50 cm²    B. 40.00 cm²    C. 39.69 cm²    D. 79.37 cm²

## Solution
The sides are sums of radii: $3+5 = 8$, $3+7 = 10$ and $5+7 = 12$ cm.
---
$s = \dfrac{8+10+12}{2} = 15$.
---
Heron: $A = \sqrt{15(7)(5)(3)} = \sqrt{1575} = 39.69$ cm².
