topic: geometry/plane-solid-analytic
title: Water in a spherical tank
answer: D
hint: The water fills a segment of the sphere. Its height is the water depth.

A spherical water tank has an inside radius of 3 m. The water in it is 1.2 m deep at the lowest point. How much water is in the tank?

A. 35.29 m³    B. 22.62 m³    C. 56.55 m³    D. 11.76 m³

## Solution
Segment volume: $V = \dfrac{\pi h^2(3R - h)}{3}$ with $R = 3$, $h = 1.2$.
---
$V = \dfrac{\pi(1.44)(9 - 1.2)}{3} = \dfrac{\pi(1.44)(7.8)}{3}$.
---
$V = 11.76$ m³.
