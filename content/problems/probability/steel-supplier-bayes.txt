topic: probability/counting-and-probability
title: Which supplier sent the bad bar
answer: B
hint: Find what share of all bars are defective and from Y, out of all defective bars.

Supplier X provides 70% of a project's steel bars and supplier Y provides 30%. Of X's bars, 1% are defective; of Y's, 4% are. A bar picked at random is defective. What is the probability it came from Y?

A. 0.300    B. 0.632    C. 0.368    D. 0.040

## Solution
Defective and from X: $0.70 \times 0.01 = 0.007$. Defective and from Y: $0.30 \times 0.04 = 0.012$.
---
All defective: $0.007 + 0.012 = 0.019$.
---
$P(Y \mid \text{defective}) = \dfrac{0.012}{0.019} = 0.632$.
