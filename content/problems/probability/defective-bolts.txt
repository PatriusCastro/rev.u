topic: probability/counting-and-probability
title: Two defective bolts
answer: C
hint: Without replacing, the second draw has one fewer bolt and one fewer defective.

A box holds 12 bolts, 3 of them defective. Two bolts are drawn at random without replacement. What is the probability that both are defective?

A. 1/16    B. 1/11    C. 1/22    D. 3/22

## Solution
First bolt defective: $\dfrac{3}{12}$.
---
Second bolt defective, given the first was: $\dfrac{2}{11}$.
---
$P = \dfrac{3}{12} \times \dfrac{2}{11} = \dfrac{6}{132} = \dfrac{1}{22}$.
