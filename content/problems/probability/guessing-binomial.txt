title: Guessing on a quiz
answer: A
hint: Ten independent tries, each right with probability 1/4. That is a binomial.

A quiz has 10 multiple-choice items, each with 4 choices. A student guesses every item. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 right?

A. 0.250    B. 0.002    C. 0.776    D. 0.300

## Solution
Binomial with $n = 10$, $p = 0.25$, $k = 3$.
---
$P(3) = C(10,3)(0.25)^3(0.75)^7 = 120(0.015625)(0.13348)$.
---
$P(3) = 0.250$.
