title: No cracks in 50 m of pavement
answer: B
hint: The rate is given per 100 m. Scale it to 50 m before using the Poisson formula.

Cracks in a pavement occur at random at an average of 2 per 100 m. What is the probability that a 50 m stretch has no cracks?

A. 0.135    B. 0.368    C. 0.632    D. 0.184

## Solution
For 50 m, the average is $\lambda = 2 \times \dfrac{50}{100} = 1$.
---
$P(0) = \dfrac{1^0 e^{-1}}{0!} = e^{-1} = 0.368$.
