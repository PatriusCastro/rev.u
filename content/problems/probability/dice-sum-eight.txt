topic: probability/counting-and-probability
title: Two dice that add to 8
answer: D
hint: Two dice give 36 equally likely pairs. List the pairs that add to 8.

Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that the numbers add to 8?

A. 1/6    B. 1/9    C. 7/36    D. 5/36

## Solution
There are $6 \times 6 = 36$ equally likely pairs.
---
Pairs adding to 8: (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2). That is 5 pairs.
---
$P = \dfrac{5}{36}$.
