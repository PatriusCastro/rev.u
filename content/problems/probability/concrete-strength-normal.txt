title: Weak concrete cylinders
answer: A
hint: Turn 25 MPa into a z-score, then find the area to its left.

The compressive strength of a concrete mix is normally distributed with a mean of 30 MPa and a standard deviation of 3 MPa. What percentage of cylinders test below 25 MPa?

A. 4.78%    B. 9.56%    C. 15.87%    D. 47.80%

## Solution
$z = \dfrac{25 - 30}{3} = -1.67$.
---
The area to the left of $z = -1.67$ is $1 - 0.9522 = 0.0478$.
---
About 4.78% of cylinders test below 25 MPa.
