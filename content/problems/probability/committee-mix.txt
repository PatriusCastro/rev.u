title: Committee with two engineers
answer: A
hint: A committee has no order. Choose the engineers and the architect separately, then multiply.

A committee of 3 is chosen from 7 engineers and 5 architects. In how many ways can it have exactly 2 engineers?

A. 105    B. 210    C. 35    D. 175

## Solution
Choose 2 of the 7 engineers: $C(7,2) = 21$.
---
Choose 1 of the 5 architects: $C(5,1) = 5$.
---
$21 \times 5 = 105$ ways.
