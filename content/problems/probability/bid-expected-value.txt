topic: probability/counting-and-probability
title: Expected value of a bid
answer: C
hint: Each outcome times its probability, then add. The lost bid cost counts as negative.

A contractor spends ₱30,000 preparing a bid. If the bid wins, which has a 40% chance, the net profit is ₱500,000. If it loses, the ₱30,000 is lost. What is the expected gain from bidding?

A. ₱200,000    B. ₱188,000    C. ₱182,000    D. ₱170,000

## Solution
Win: $0.40 \times 500{,}000 = 200{,}000$.
---
Lose: $0.60 \times (-30{,}000) = -18{,}000$.
---
Expected gain $= 200{,}000 - 18{,}000 = ₱182{,}000$.
