topic: probability/counting-and-probability
title: Site uniforms
answer: C
hint: One choice of each kind, all together: multiply.

A site engineer has 4 shirts, 3 pairs of pants and 2 pairs of safety shoes. How many different outfits of one shirt, one pair of pants and one pair of shoes can be made?

A. 9    B. 12    C. 24    D. 36

## Solution
Each outfit is one shirt, one pair of pants and one pair of shoes, chosen together, so multiply.
---
$4 \times 3 \times 2 = 24$ outfits.
