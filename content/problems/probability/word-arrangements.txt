topic: probability/counting-and-probability
title: Arranging the letters of LEVEL
answer: B
hint: Some letters repeat. Divide by the factorial of each repeat count.

How many different arrangements can be made from all the letters of the word LEVEL?

A. 120    B. 30    C. 60    D. 20

## Solution
LEVEL has 5 letters: L twice, E twice and V once.
---
$\dfrac{5!}{2!\,2!} = \dfrac{120}{4} = 30$ arrangements.
