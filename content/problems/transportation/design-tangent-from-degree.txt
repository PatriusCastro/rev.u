topic: transportation/highway-design
title: Tangent length from the degree of curve
answer: A
hint: Get the radius from the degree of curve first. The tangent uses half the intersection angle.

A provincial road in Bukidnon turns through an intersection angle of 38° on a simple curve with a degree of curve of 4°30'. The degree of curve is defined on a 20 m arc, so $D = \dfrac{1145.916}{R}$. Find the tangent length.

A. 87.68 m    B. 198.95 m    C. 168.89 m    D. 438.41 m

## Solution
$D = 4.5°$, so $R = \dfrac{1145.916}{4.5} = 254.65$ m.
---
$T = R\tan\dfrac{I}{2} = 254.65\tan 19°$.
---
$T = 254.65(0.34433) = 87.68$ m.
