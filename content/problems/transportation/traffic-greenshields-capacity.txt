topic: transportation/traffic
title: Capacity of an expressway lane
answer: C
hint: Read the free-flow speed and the jam density from the equation. At capacity both speed and density are half their limits.

Speed studies on one lane of an expressway in Laguna fit the Greenshields relation $v = 90 - 0.6k$, with $v$ in km/h and $k$ in veh/km. What is the capacity of the lane?

A. 6750 veh/h    B. 13500 veh/h    C. 3375 veh/h    D. 1688 veh/h

## Solution
Free-flow speed: $v_f = 90$ km/h. Jam density: $0 = 90 - 0.6k_j$, so $k_j = 150$ veh/km.
---
At capacity $k = 75$ veh/km and $v = 45$ km/h.
---
$q_{max} = kv = 75 \times 45 = 3375$ veh/h. Check: $\dfrac{v_f k_j}{4} = \dfrac{90(150)}{4} = 3375$.
