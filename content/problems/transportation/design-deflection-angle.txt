topic: transportation/highway-design
title: Deflection angle to a curve station
answer: D
hint: Find the arc length from the PC to the point. The deflection angle is half the central angle that arc subtends.

A simple curve of radius 200 m begins at the PC, station 2+120.00. A surveyor sets up a total station at the PC, sighted along the back tangent extended forward. What deflection angle should be turned to set the stake at station 2+200.00?

A. 22°55'06"    B. 5°43'46"    C. 2°51'53"    D. 11°27'33"

## Solution
Arc length from the PC: $s = 2200.00 - 2120.00 = 80.00$ m.
---
Central angle: $\dfrac{s}{R} = \dfrac{80}{200} = 0.4$ rad $= 22.918°$.
---
The deflection angle is half: $\delta = 11.459° = 11°27'33"$.
