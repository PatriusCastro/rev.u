topic: transportation/highway-design
title: Minimum radius of a curve
answer: A
hint: At the minimum radius both the superelevation and the side friction are at their limits. Add them.

An expressway in Central Luzon has a design speed of 100 km/h. The maximum superelevation is 0.08 and the side friction factor is 0.12. Using $R = \dfrac{v^2}{127(e + f)}$ with $v$ in km/h, find the minimum radius.

A. 393.70 m    B. 984.25 m    C. 656.17 m    D. 196.85 m

## Solution
$e + f = 0.08 + 0.12 = 0.20$.
---
$R = \dfrac{100^2}{127(0.20)} = \dfrac{10{,}000}{25.4}$.
---
$R = 393.70$ m.
