topic: transportation/traffic
title: Peak hour factor on an approach
answer: B
hint: The hourly volume is the sum of the four counts. Compare it with four times one of the counts.

The 15-minute counts on an approach to a Quezon City intersection during the morning peak hour are 410, 465, 520 and 445 vehicles. Find the peak hour factor.

A. 0.283    B. 0.885    C. 1.122    D. 0.989

## Solution
Hourly volume: $V = 410 + 465 + 520 + 445 = 1840$ veh.
---
Highest 15-minute count: 520 veh.
---
$PHF = \dfrac{1840}{4(520)} = 0.885$.
