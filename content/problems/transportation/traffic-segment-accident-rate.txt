topic: transportation/traffic
title: Accident rate on a highway segment
answer: D
hint: A segment rate is per vehicle-kilometre, so the length belongs in the denominator once.

A 12.5 km stretch of a national highway in Pampanga carries an AADT of 15,000 vehicles. In one year it had 46 accidents. Find the accident rate per 100 million vehicle-km, using $R = \dfrac{A \times 10^8}{365\,T\,V L}$.

A. 134.4    B. 840.2    C. 5.38    D. 67.2

## Solution
Vehicle-km in one year: $365(1)(15{,}000)(12.5) = 68.44 \times 10^6$ veh-km.
---
$R = \dfrac{46 \times 10^8}{68.44 \times 10^6} = 67.2$ accidents per 100 million veh-km.
