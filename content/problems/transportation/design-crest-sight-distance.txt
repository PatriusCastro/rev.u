title: Sight distance on a crest curve
answer: A
hint: Get the curve length from the rate of change of grade first. Then solve the given formula for $S$ and confirm it is shorter than the curve.

A crest vertical curve joins a +3.5% grade to a −2.5% grade. The grade changes at 0.4% per 20 m station. Assuming the sight distance is shorter than the curve, use $L = \dfrac{AS^2}{658}$, with $A$ the algebraic difference of grades in percent, to find the available sight distance.

A. 181.38 m    B. 40.56 m    C. 444.30 m    D. 128.26 m

## Solution
$A = 3.5 - (-2.5) = 6$ percent.
---
$L = \dfrac{6}{0.4} \times 20 = 300$ m.
---
$S = \sqrt{\dfrac{658L}{A}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{658(300)}{6}} = \sqrt{32{,}900} = 181.38$ m.
---
$S = 181.38$ m is less than $L = 300$ m, so the formula applies.
