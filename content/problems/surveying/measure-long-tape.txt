topic: surveying/measurements
title: Measuring with a long tape
answer: C
hint: A tape that is longer than its marks fits fewer times into the line. Does that make the recorded length too big or too small?

A 50 m tape is checked against a baseline and found to be 50.012 m long. With this tape a road centerline is recorded as 362.48 m. How much must be added to the recorded length to get the true length? (A negative value means subtract.)

A. −0.087 m    B. +0.145 m    C. +0.087 m    D. +0.012 m

## Solution
True length $= 362.48 \times \dfrac{50.012}{50} = 362.567$ m.
---
Correction $= 362.567 - 362.48 = +0.087$ m.
---
Check: the error is 0.012 m per tape length and the line is $362.48/50 = 7.25$ tape lengths, so $7.25 \times 0.012 = 0.087$ m.
