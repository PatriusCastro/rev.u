topic: surveying/traverse-areas
title: Precision of a closed traverse
answer: A
hint: The two closing errors are at right angles to each other.

A closed traverse around a subdivision in Laguna has a perimeter of 1,248.5 m. The sum of the latitudes is +0.38 m and the sum of the departures is −0.29 m. What is the relative precision?

A. 1 in 2,612    B. 1 in 3,286    C. 1 in 1,863    D. 1 in 5,464

## Solution
Linear error of closure: $\text{LEC} = \sqrt{0.38^2 + (-0.29)^2} = \sqrt{0.2285} = 0.478$ m.
---
Relative precision $= \dfrac{\text{LEC}}{P} = \dfrac{0.478}{1248.5} = \dfrac{1}{2612}$.
---
The traverse closes to 1 in 2,612.
