topic: surveying/traverse-areas
title: Lot area from coordinates
answer: D
hint: List the corners in order around the lot and repeat the first one at the end.

The corners of a lot in Cavite have these coordinates in metres (x east, y north), listed in order around the boundary: A (0.00, 0.00), B (86.40, 12.50), C (94.20, 71.80), D (18.60, 63.00). What is the area of the lot?

A. 9,625.14 m²    B. 6,069.06 m²    C. 2,406.29 m²    D. 4,812.57 m²

## Solution
Shoelace: $2A = \sum (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)$.
---
A to B: $0(12.50) - 86.40(0) = 0$. B to C: $86.40(71.80) - 94.20(12.50) = 6203.52 - 1177.50 = 5026.02$.
---
C to D: $94.20(63.00) - 18.60(71.80) = 5934.60 - 1335.48 = 4599.12$. D to A: $18.60(0) - 0(63.00) = 0$.
---
$2A = 9625.14$, so $A = 4812.57$ m².
