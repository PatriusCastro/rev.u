title: Compass rule on one course
answer: C
hint: The correction is shared in proportion to course length, and it works against the error.

A five-course closed traverse has a perimeter of 1,480.0 m. The departures add up to +0.42 m. Using the compass (Bowditch) rule, what correction should be applied to the departure of course CD, which is 312.4 m long?

A. +0.089 m    B. −0.084 m    C. −0.089 m    D. −0.420 m

## Solution
Compass rule: $c_{\text{dep}} = -\sum\text{Dep}\cdot\dfrac{L}{P}$.
---
$c_{\text{dep}} = -0.42 \times \dfrac{312.4}{1480.0} = -0.42 \times 0.2111 = -0.0887$ m.
---
The correction is −0.089 m. It is negative because the departures add up to too much.
