topic: structural/construction-management
title: Present worth of lease payments
answer: D
hint: Each payment is made at year end, so discount the whole series back to today.

A firm in Davao will pay ₱120,000 at the end of each year for 6 years to lease a compactor. Money is worth 10% per year. Use $P = A\,\dfrac{(1+i)^n - 1}{i(1+i)^n}$. What single amount today is equal to these payments?

A. ₱720,000    B. ₱925,873    C. ₱574,894    D. ₱522,631

## Solution
$(1.10)^6 = 1.771561$.
---
Factor: $\dfrac{1.771561 - 1}{0.10(1.771561)} = \dfrac{0.771561}{0.1771561} = 4.35526$.
---
$P = 120{,}000(4.35526) =$ ₱522,631.
