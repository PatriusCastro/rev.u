topic: structural/design-construction
title: Factored moment in a one-way slab
answer: D
hint: Work on a 1 m wide strip. The dead load includes the slab's own weight as well as the floor finish.

A one-way slab in a townhouse in Cavite is 150 mm thick and simply supported over a clear span of 3.6 m. It carries a floor finish and ceiling of 1.5 kPa and a live load of 2.4 kPa. Concrete weighs 24 kN/m³. Per NSCP 2015, use the factored load $w_u = 1.2D + 1.6L$ and $M_u = \dfrac{w_u L^2}{8}$ for a 1 m strip. Find $M_u$ per metre width.

A. 9.14 kN·m    B. 12.15 kN·m    C. 18.18 kN·m    D. 16.14 kN·m

## Solution
Dead load on a 1 m strip: $D = 0.15(24) + 1.5 = 3.6 + 1.5 = 5.1$ kN/m. Live load: $L = 2.4$ kN/m.
---
$w_u = 1.2(5.1) + 1.6(2.4) = 6.12 + 3.84 = 9.96$ kN/m.
---
$M_u = \dfrac{9.96(3.6)^2}{8} = 16.14$ kN·m per metre width.
