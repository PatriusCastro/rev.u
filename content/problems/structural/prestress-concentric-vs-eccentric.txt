topic: structural/prestressed
title: Concentric versus eccentric prestress
answer: B
hint: For each case find the moment that just brings the bottom fibre to zero stress, then turn that moment into a uniform load on the span.

Two identical simply supported beams, each 300 mm wide and 600 mm deep and spanning 10 m, carry an effective prestress of 1200 kN. In beam 1 the tendon is at the centroid. In beam 2 it is straight, 180 mm below the centroid. No tension is allowed at the bottom fibre. How much more total uniform load (self-weight included) can beam 2 carry than beam 1?

A. 26.88 kN/m    B. 17.28 kN/m    C. 9.60 kN/m    D. 8.64 kN/m

## Solution
For a rectangle the kern distance is $h/6 = 100$ mm.
---
Beam 1: $M = P(h/6) = 1200(0.10) = 120$ kN·m, so $w = \dfrac{8M}{L^2} = \dfrac{8(120)}{10^2} = 9.60$ kN/m.
---
Beam 2: $M = P(h/6 + e) = 1200(0.28) = 336$ kN·m, so $w = \dfrac{8(336)}{10^2} = 26.88$ kN/m.
---
Difference $= 26.88 - 9.60 = 17.28$ kN/m.
