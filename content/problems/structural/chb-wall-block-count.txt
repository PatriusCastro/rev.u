topic: structural/design-construction
title: Hollow blocks for a wall with openings
answer: A
hint: Count blocks on the net wall area. Deduct every opening before multiplying.

A concrete hollow block wall is 8 m long and 3 m high. It has one door opening 0.9 m wide by 2.1 m high and one window opening 1.2 m wide by 1.0 m high. Take 12.5 hollow blocks per square metre of wall. How many blocks are needed?

A. 262 blocks    B. 300 blocks    C. 277 blocks    D. 210 blocks

## Solution
Gross area: $8(3) = 24$ m².
---
Openings: $0.9(2.1) + 1.2(1.0) = 1.89 + 1.20 = 3.09$ m².
---
Net area: $24 - 3.09 = 20.91$ m².
---
Blocks: $20.91(12.5) = 261.4$, so 262 blocks.
