topic: structural/design-construction
title: Balanced steel ratio
answer: C
hint: The balanced ratio has two parts: the concrete block term with β1, and the strain term 600/(600 + fy).

A designer checks the reinforcement limits for beams in a small warehouse. The concrete has $f'_c = 21$ MPa and the bars are Grade 40 with $f_y = 276$ MPa. Per NSCP 2015, take $\beta_1 = 0.85$ and $E_s = 200{,}000$ MPa (so the strain term is $\dfrac{600}{600 + f_y}$). Find the balanced steel ratio $\rho_b$.

A. 0.0443    B. 0.0282    C. 0.0377    D. 0.0173

## Solution
$\rho_b = \dfrac{0.85\beta_1 f'_c}{f_y}\cdot\dfrac{600}{600 + f_y}$.
---
$\dfrac{0.85(0.85)(21)}{276} = 0.05497$ and $\dfrac{600}{600 + 276} = 0.6849$.
---
$\rho_b = 0.05497(0.6849) = 0.0377$.
