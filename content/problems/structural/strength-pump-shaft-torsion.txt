topic: structural/statics-mechanics
title: Shear stress in a pump shaft
answer: A
hint: Turn rpm into revolutions per second before finding the torque.

A solid steel shaft 60 mm in diameter drives an irrigation pump. It transmits 75 kW at 300 rpm. Find the maximum shear stress in the shaft.

A. 56.3 MPa    B. 112.6 MPa    C. 353.7 MPa    D. 28.1 MPa

## Solution
$f = \dfrac{300}{60} = 5$ rev/s.
---
$T = \dfrac{P}{2\pi f} = \dfrac{75\,000}{2\pi(5)} = 2387$ N·m $= 2.387 \times 10^6$ N·mm.
---
$\tau = \dfrac{16T}{\pi d^3} = \dfrac{16(2.387 \times 10^6)}{\pi(60)^3} = 56.3$ MPa.
