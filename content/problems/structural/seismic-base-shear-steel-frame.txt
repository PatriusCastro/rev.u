topic: structural/seismic
title: Base shear of a five-storey steel frame
answer: A
hint: Compute $T$ first, then the formula value of $V$, then compare it with every limit.

A 5-storey steel moment-resisting frame in Zone 4 ($Z = 0.4$) stands on soil profile SD. The roof is 17.5 m above the base and the seismic weight is $W = 8000$ kN. Use $C_a = 0.44$, $C_v = 0.64$, $N_v = 1.0$, $I = 1.0$, $R = 8.5$ and $C_t = 0.0853$. Per NSCP 2015, $V = \dfrac{C_v I W}{R T}$, with $V \le \dfrac{2.5 C_a I W}{R}$, $V \ge 0.11 C_a I W$ and $V \ge \dfrac{0.8 Z N_v I W}{R}$. Find the design base shear.

A. 825.3 kN    B. 1035.3 kN    C. 963.0 kN    D. 387.2 kN

## Solution
$T = 0.0853(17.5)^{3/4} = 0.730$ s.
---
Formula: $V = \dfrac{0.64(1.0)(8000)}{8.5(0.730)} = 825.3$ kN.
---
Upper limit: $\dfrac{2.5(0.44)(1.0)(8000)}{8.5} = 1035.3$ kN. This is above 825.3 kN, so it does not cap $V$.
---
Lower limits: $0.11(0.44)(1.0)(8000) = 387.2$ kN and $\dfrac{0.8(0.4)(1.0)(1.0)(8000)}{8.5} = 301.2$ kN. Both are below 825.3 kN.
---
The formula value governs: $V = 825.3$ kN.
