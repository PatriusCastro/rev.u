topic: structural/statics-mechanics
title: Steel rail with an expansion gap
answer: A
hint: First find how much the rail would grow if it were free. Only the part beyond the gap is stopped.

A steel rail 12 m long is laid on a hot afternoon in Pampanga between two rigid stops, with a gap of 1.5 mm left at one end. The temperature then rises by a further 25°C. Take $\alpha = 11.7 \times 10^{-6}$ per °C and $E = 200$ GPa. Find the compressive stress in the rail.

A. 33.5 MPa    B. 58.5 MPa    C. 25.0 MPa    D. 83.5 MPa

## Solution
Free expansion: $\delta_T = \alpha L \Delta T = 11.7 \times 10^{-6}(12\,000)(25) = 3.51$ mm.
---
The gap takes 1.5 mm, so $3.51 - 1.5 = 2.01$ mm is prevented.
---
$\sigma = \dfrac{E\delta}{L} = \dfrac{200\,000(2.01)}{12\,000} = 33.5$ MPa.
