topic: structural/timber
title: Reaction allowed at a notched support
answer: B
hint: The notch reduces the depth and also adds a stress-raising factor of d over the remaining depth.

An apitong joist is dressed 100 mm wide and 300 mm deep. At one support its bottom (tension) face is notched so that 240 mm of depth remains. Allowable shear stress $F_v = 1.0$ MPa. Use $f_v = \dfrac{3V}{2bd_n}\left(\dfrac{d}{d_n}\right)$. Find the largest end reaction it can take.

A. 16.00 kN    B. 12.80 kN    C. 10.24 kN    D. 20.00 kN

## Solution
Solve for $V$: $V = \dfrac{2F_v b d_n}{3}\left(\dfrac{d_n}{d}\right)$.
---
$V = \dfrac{2(1.0)(100)(240)}{3}\left(\dfrac{240}{300}\right) = 16\,000(0.8) = 12\,800$ N.
---
$V = 12.80$ kN.
