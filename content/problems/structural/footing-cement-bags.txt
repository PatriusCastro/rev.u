topic: structural/design-construction
title: Cement bags for isolated footings
answer: D
hint: Find the concrete volume of one footing, multiply by the number of footings, then use the bags per cubic metre. You cannot buy part of a bag.

A two-storey house in Batangas has 12 identical isolated footings, each 1.8 m by 1.8 m in plan and 0.45 m thick. The footings use a Class A mix, taken as 9.0 bags of 40 kg cement per cubic metre of concrete. How many bags of cement are needed?

A. 132 bags    B. 14 bags    C. 175 bags    D. 158 bags

## Solution
Volume of one footing: $1.8(1.8)(0.45) = 1.458$ m³.
---
Total volume: $12(1.458) = 17.496$ m³.
---
Cement: $17.496(9.0) = 157.5$ bags, so order 158 bags.
