topic: structural/timber
title: Load allowed by the deflection limit
answer: C
hint: Set the midspan deflection of a uniformly loaded simple span equal to L/360 and solve for w.

A tanguile beam, dressed 100 mm wide and 250 mm deep, spans 4.5 m simply supported. $E = 10\,000$ MPa. Deflection is limited to $L/360$. What uniform load makes the midspan deflection reach the limit?

A. 4.57 kN/m    B. 15.24 kN/m    C. 3.05 kN/m    D. 12.19 kN/m

## Solution
$\Delta_{allow} = \dfrac{4500}{360} = 12.5$ mm.
---
$I = \dfrac{bd^3}{12} = \dfrac{100(250)^3}{12} = 130.21 \times 10^6$ mm⁴.
---
$w = \dfrac{384EI\Delta}{5L^4} = \dfrac{384(10\,000)(130.21 \times 10^6)(12.5)}{5(4500)^4} = 3.05$ N/mm $= 3.05$ kN/m.
