topic: structural/construction-management
title: Formwork for columns
answer: C
hint: The forms wrap all four faces of each column. The top and bottom are not formed.

A warehouse has 12 columns, each 0.40 m by 0.50 m in section, with a formed height of 3.0 m. Formwork area for one column is its perimeter times its height. What total formwork contact area is needed?

A. 32.4 m²    B. 69.6 m²    C. 64.8 m²    D. 7.2 m²

## Solution
Perimeter: $2(0.40 + 0.50) = 1.80$ m.
---
One column: $1.80 \times 3.0 = 5.40$ m².
---
Total: $12 \times 5.40 = 64.8$ m².
