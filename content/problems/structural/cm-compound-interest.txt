title: Loan for a mixer
answer: B
hint: The interest earns interest each year, so raise the growth factor to the number of years.

A contractor borrows ₱500,000 to buy a concrete mixer at 8% interest compounded annually. Use $F = P(1 + i)^n$. How much does the contractor owe at the end of 5 years if nothing is paid before then?

A. ₱700,000    B. ₱734,664    C. ₱793,437    D. ₱680,245

## Solution
$(1.08)^5 = 1.46933$.
---
$F = 500{,}000(1.46933) =$ ₱734,664.
---
Simple interest would give only $500{,}000(1 + 0.08 \times 5) =$ ₱700,000.
