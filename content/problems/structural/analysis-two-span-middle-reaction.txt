topic: structural/analysis
title: Middle support of a two-span beam
answer: C
hint: The middle support of a continuous beam takes more than its tributary share.

A continuous floor beam in a school building rests on three simple supports, giving two equal spans of 5 m each. It carries a uniform load of 24 kN/m over both spans. EI is constant. Find the reaction at the middle support.

A. 45 kN    B. 120 kN    C. 150 kN    D. 180 kN

## Solution
For two equal spans with uniform load on both, the moment over the middle support is $\dfrac{wL^2}{8} = \dfrac{24(5)^2}{8} = 75$ kN·m, hogging.
---
End reaction, taking moments of span 1 about the middle support: $R_1(5) = \dfrac{24(5)^2}{2} - 75 = 225$, so $R_1 = 45$ kN $= 0.375wL$.
---
Middle reaction: $R_2 = 2wL - 2R_1 = 240 - 90 = 150$ kN $= 1.25wL$.
