topic: structural/analysis
title: Maximum midspan moment from live loads
answer: B
hint: The influence line for midspan moment is a triangle. Find its peak and its area.

A simply supported footbridge girder spans 12 m. It carries a moving 40 kN point load and a uniform live load of 6 kN/m that may cover any part of the span. Using the influence line for the moment at midspan, find the maximum midspan moment from these live loads.

A. 174 kN·m    B. 228 kN·m    C. 336 kN·m    D. 348 kN·m

## Solution
The influence line for midspan moment is a triangle with peak $\dfrac{L}{4} = \dfrac{12}{4} = 3$ m at midspan.
---
Point load at midspan: $40 \times 3 = 120$ kN·m.
---
Uniform load over the whole span: $6 \times \text{area} = 6 \times \dfrac{12(3)}{2} = 6 \times 18 = 108$ kN·m.
---
Maximum midspan moment: $120 + 108 = 228$ kN·m.
