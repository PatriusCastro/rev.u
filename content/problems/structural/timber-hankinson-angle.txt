topic: structural/timber
title: Allowable stress at an angle to the grain
answer: B
hint: Check your formula at θ = 0: it must give the stress parallel to the grain.

A sloping rafter of apitong bears on a tie beam so that the load acts at 35° to the grain of the tie beam. Allowable compression parallel to grain $F_c = 11$ MPa and perpendicular to grain $F_{c\perp} = 3.0$ MPa. Using Hankinson's formula, find the allowable compressive stress at this angle.

A. 3.94 MPa    B. 5.86 MPa    C. 8.37 MPa    D. 3.76 MPa

## Solution
Hankinson: $F_n = \dfrac{F_c F_{c\perp}}{F_c \sin^2\theta + F_{c\perp}\cos^2\theta}$.
---
$F_c\sin^2 35° = 11(0.3290) = 3.619$ and $F_{c\perp}\cos^2 35° = 3.0(0.6710) = 2.013$.
---
$F_n = \dfrac{11(3.0)}{3.619 + 2.013} = 5.86$ MPa.
