topic: structural/statics-mechanics
title: Bending stress in a timber joist
answer: C
hint: The depth is the dimension in the direction of the load. It is the one that gets cubed.

A rectangular timber joist is 150 mm wide and 300 mm deep and is bent about its strong axis by a moment of 45 kN·m. Find the maximum bending stress.

A. 40 MPa    B. 10 MPa    C. 20 MPa    D. 5 MPa

## Solution
$I = \dfrac{bh^3}{12} = \dfrac{150(300)^3}{12} = 337.5 \times 10^6$ mm⁴, and $c = 150$ mm.
---
$\sigma = \dfrac{Mc}{I} = \dfrac{45 \times 10^6 (150)}{337.5 \times 10^6}$.
---
$\sigma = 20$ MPa.
