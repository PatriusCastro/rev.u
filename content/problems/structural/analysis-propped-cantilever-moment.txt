topic: structural/analysis
title: Fixed-end moment of a propped cantilever
answer: C
hint: Find the roller reaction of a propped cantilever first, then take moments about the fixed end.

A 5 m reinforced concrete beam is fixed into a wall at one end and rests on a roller support at the other. It carries a uniform load of 16 kN/m over its whole length. EI is constant. Find the magnitude of the moment at the fixed end.

A. 28.13 kN·m    B. 33.33 kN·m    C. 50.00 kN·m    D. 66.67 kN·m

## Solution
For a propped cantilever with uniform load, the roller reaction is $R = \dfrac{3wL}{8} = \dfrac{3(16)(5)}{8} = 30$ kN.
---
Moment at the fixed end: $M = RL - \dfrac{wL^2}{2} = 30(5) - \dfrac{16(5)^2}{2} = 150 - 200 = -50$ kN·m.
---
This matches $\dfrac{wL^2}{8} = \dfrac{16(25)}{8} = 50$ kN·m, hogging.
