topic: structural/steel
title: Uniform load on a compact beam
answer: B
hint: Find the design moment of the section, then set it equal to the maximum moment of a simple span under uniform load.

A floor beam in a Makati office building is simply supported over 8.0 m. It is compact and its compression flange is fully braced. Its plastic section modulus is $Z_x = 1.65 \times 10^6$ mm³ and $F_y = 345$ MPa. Find the largest factored uniform load $w_u$ the beam can carry in flexure, with $\phi = 0.90$.

A. 71.16 kN/m    B. 64.04 kN/m    C. 80.05 kN/m    D. 96.06 kN/m

## Solution
$M_p = F_yZ_x = 345(1.65 \times 10^6) = 569.3$ kN·m.
---
$\phi M_n = 0.90(569.3) = 512.3$ kN·m.
---
$\dfrac{w_uL^2}{8} = 512.3$, so $w_u = \dfrac{8(512.3)}{8.0^2} = 64.04$ kN/m.
