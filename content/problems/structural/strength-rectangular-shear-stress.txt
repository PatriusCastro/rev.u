topic: structural/statics-mechanics
title: Peak shear stress in a rectangular beam
answer: B
hint: Shear stress is not uniform over the depth. The peak at the neutral axis is larger than the average.

A rectangular concrete beam section is 200 mm wide and 400 mm deep. At a support the vertical shear is 120 kN. Treat the section as homogeneous and uncracked. Find the maximum shear stress.

A. 1.50 MPa    B. 2.25 MPa    C. 3.00 MPa    D. 4.50 MPa

## Solution
$A = 200 \times 400 = 80\,000$ mm².
---
For a rectangle, $\tau_{max} = \dfrac{3V}{2A} = \dfrac{1.5(120\,000)}{80\,000}$.
---
$\tau_{max} = 2.25$ MPa, at the neutral axis.
