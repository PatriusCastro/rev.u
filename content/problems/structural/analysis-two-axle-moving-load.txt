topic: structural/analysis
title: Absolute maximum moment from a truck
answer: C
hint: Find where the resultant of the two axles acts, then let midspan split the gap between it and the heavier axle.

A two-axle truck crosses a simply supported bridge span of 10 m. The rear axle carries 120 kN and the front axle 80 kN, spaced 5 m apart. Find the absolute maximum bending moment in the span. Use the rule: the maximum moment occurs under the heavier axle when midspan bisects the distance between that axle and the resultant of the axles.

A. 300 kN·m    B. 180 kN·m    C. 320 kN·m    D. 500 kN·m

## Solution
Resultant: $R = 120 + 80 = 200$ kN, located $d = \dfrac{80(5)}{200} = 2$ m from the 120 kN axle.
---
Place the 120 kN axle $\dfrac{d}{2} = 1$ m to one side of midspan, 4 m from the left support. The resultant is then 1 m to the other side, 6 m from the left support. The 80 kN axle is at 9 m, still on the span.
---
Left reaction: $R_L = \dfrac{200(10 - 6)}{10} = 80$ kN.
---
Moment under the 120 kN axle: $M = 80(4) = 320$ kN·m.
