topic: structural/timber
title: Bearing length at a wall support
answer: D
hint: The beam presses on its support across the grain. Use the dressed width.

A tanguile girder, nominal 100 mm wide and dressed 90 mm wide, rests on a concrete wall. Its end reaction is 42 kN. Allowable compression parallel to grain is 9.5 MPa and perpendicular to grain is 2.4 MPa. Find the minimum bearing length.

A. 175.0 mm    B. 97.2 mm    C. 49.1 mm    D. 194.4 mm

## Solution
Bearing on the support is perpendicular to the grain, so use $F_{c\perp} = 2.4$ MPa.
---
Required length $= \dfrac{R}{F_{c\perp} b} = \dfrac{42\,000}{2.4(90)} = 194.4$ mm.
