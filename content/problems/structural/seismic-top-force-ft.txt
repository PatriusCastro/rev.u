topic: structural/seismic
title: Extra force at the top of a steel frame
answer: A
hint: Find $T$ first. $F_t$ applies only above a certain period, and it has a cap.

A 5-storey steel moment-resisting frame has its roof 18.0 m above the base and a design base shear $V = 1200$ kN. Use $T = 0.0853 h_n^{3/4}$ ($h_n$ in m). Per NSCP 2015, $F_t = 0.07 T V \le 0.25V$ when $T > 0.7$ s, and $F_t = 0$ otherwise. Find $F_t$.

A. 62.6 kN    B. 84.0 kN    C. 300.0 kN    D. 0 kN

## Solution
$T = 0.0853(18.0)^{3/4} = 0.745$ s. This is above 0.7 s, so $F_t$ applies.
---
$F_t = 0.07(0.745)(1200) = 62.6$ kN.
---
Cap: $0.25(1200) = 300$ kN. The value 62.6 kN is below the cap, so $F_t = 62.6$ kN.
