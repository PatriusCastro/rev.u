topic: structural/statics-mechanics
title: Stretch of a steel hanger rod
answer: D
hint: Work in N and mm. The area uses the radius, not the diameter.

A solid round steel hanger rod 25 mm in diameter and 3 m long supports a tension of 80 kN. Take $E = 200$ GPa. How much does the rod stretch?

A. 0.61 mm    B. 1.92 mm    C. 4.89 mm    D. 2.44 mm

## Solution
$A = \dfrac{\pi(25)^2}{4} = 490.9$ mm².
---
$\delta = \dfrac{PL}{AE} = \dfrac{80\,000(3000)}{490.9(200\,000)}$.
---
$\delta = 2.44$ mm.
