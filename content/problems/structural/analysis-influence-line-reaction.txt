topic: structural/analysis
title: Maximum reaction from a moving load
answer: A
hint: Put the point load where the influence line for the reaction is highest, and the uniform load wherever its ordinates are positive.

A simply supported bridge girder spans 10 m. It carries a moving 50 kN point load and a uniform live load of 8 kN/m that may cover any part of the span. Using the influence line for the left reaction, find the maximum left reaction from these live loads.

A. 90 kN    B. 65 kN    C. 70 kN    D. 130 kN

## Solution
The influence line for the left reaction runs from 1 at the left support to 0 at the right support.
---
Point load at the left support: $50 \times 1 = 50$ kN.
---
Uniform load over the whole span: $8 \times \text{area} = 8 \times \dfrac{1(10)}{2} = 40$ kN.
---
Maximum left reaction: $50 + 40 = 90$ kN.
