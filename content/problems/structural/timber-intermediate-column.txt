topic: structural/timber
title: Capacity of an intermediate timber column
answer: C
hint: Check which range L/d falls in before choosing a formula. The intermediate formula uses a fourth power.

A yakal column is dressed 140 mm × 140 mm with an effective length of 2.5 m. $F_c = 11$ MPa and $E = 10\,000$ MPa. Use $K = 0.671\sqrt{E/F_c}$. For $11 < L_e/d \le K$, $F_c' = F_c\left[1 - \dfrac{1}{3}\left(\dfrac{L_e/d}{K}\right)^4\right]$. Find the axial capacity.

A. 215.6 kN    B. 159.6 kN    C. 172.0 kN    D. 184.4 kN

## Solution
$\dfrac{L_e}{d} = \dfrac{2500}{140} = 17.86$, and $K = 0.671\sqrt{\dfrac{10\,000}{11}} = 20.23$.
---
$11 < 17.86 \le 20.23$, so the column is intermediate.
---
$\left(\dfrac{17.86}{20.23}\right)^4 = 0.607$, so $F_c' = 11\left[1 - \dfrac{0.607}{3}\right] = 8.775$ MPa.
---
$P = 8.775(140)(140) = 172\,000$ N $= 172.0$ kN.
