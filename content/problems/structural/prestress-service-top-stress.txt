topic: structural/prestressed
title: Top fibre stress at service after losses
answer: B
hint: Reduce the initial force by the losses first, and include the girder's own weight in the service moment.

A post-tensioned roof girder is 350 mm wide and 750 mm deep and spans 12 m, simply supported. The initial prestress is 1800 kN and total losses are 18%. The tendon sits 250 mm below the centroid along the whole span. Besides its own weight (concrete 23.5 kN/m³), the girder carries a superimposed service load of 22 kN/m. Take compression as negative. Find the top fibre stress at midspan under full service load.

A. 8.60 MPa compression    B. 9.83 MPa compression    C. 6.45 MPa compression    D. 14.22 MPa compression

## Solution
Effective prestress: $P_e = 1800(1 - 0.18) = 1476$ kN.
---
$A = 262\,500$ mm², $S = \dfrac{350(750)^2}{6} = 32.81 \times 10^6$ mm³.
---
Self-weight $= 23.5(0.35)(0.75) = 6.17$ kN/m. Total $w = 28.17$ kN/m, so $M = \dfrac{28.17(12)^2}{8} = 507.0$ kN·m.
---
$f_{top} = -\dfrac{1\,476\,000}{262\,500} + \dfrac{1\,476\,000(250)}{32.81 \times 10^6} - \dfrac{507.0 \times 10^6}{32.81 \times 10^6} = -5.62 + 11.25 - 15.45$
---
$f_{top} = -9.83$ MPa, which is 9.83 MPa compression.
