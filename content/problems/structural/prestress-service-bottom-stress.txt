topic: structural/prestressed
title: Bottom fibre stress at service
answer: C
hint: Use the effective force, and remember the eccentric prestress compresses the bottom while the load stretches it.

A precast bridge beam is 400 mm wide and 800 mm deep and spans 14 m, simply supported. The initial prestress is 2400 kN with 20% total losses, and the tendon is 280 mm below the centroid throughout. Concrete weighs 24 kN/m³, and the superimposed service load is 18 kN/m. Take compression as negative. Find the bottom fibre stress at midspan under full service load.

A. 8.50 MPa compression    B. 8.74 MPa tension    C. 3.85 MPa compression    D. 8.26 MPa compression

## Solution
$P_e = 2400(0.80) = 1920$ kN.
---
$A = 320\,000$ mm², $S = \dfrac{400(800)^2}{6} = 42.67 \times 10^6$ mm³.
---
Self-weight $= 24(0.4)(0.8) = 7.68$ kN/m. Total $w = 25.68$ kN/m, so $M = \dfrac{25.68(14)^2}{8} = 629.2$ kN·m.
---
$f_{bot} = -\dfrac{1\,920\,000}{320\,000} - \dfrac{1\,920\,000(280)}{42.67 \times 10^6} + \dfrac{629.2 \times 10^6}{42.67 \times 10^6} = -6.00 - 12.60 + 14.75$
---
$f_{bot} = -3.85$ MPa, which is 3.85 MPa compression.
