title: Unit cost of dozing
answer: D
hint: Divide the hourly cost by what the dozer actually moves in an hour, not its rated output.

A bulldozer clearing a subdivision site in Cavite is rated at 80 m³/h but works at 75% efficiency. Its owning and operating cost is ₱3,150 per hour and the operator is paid ₱450 per hour. What is the cost per cubic meter moved?

A. ₱45.00    B. ₱52.50    C. ₱80.00    D. ₱60.00

## Solution
Actual output: $80(0.75) = 60$ m³/h.
---
Hourly cost: $3150 + 450 = 3600$ pesos per hour.
---
Unit cost $= \dfrac{3600}{60} =$ ₱60.00 per m³.
