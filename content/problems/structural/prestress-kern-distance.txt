topic: structural/prestressed
title: Lower kern of an I-girder
answer: A
hint: Find the radius of gyration squared first. The lower kern point is found by dividing by the distance to the opposite (top) fibre.

A precast I-girder has a gross area of 180 000 mm² and a moment of inertia of $1.2 \times 10^{10}$ mm⁴ about its centroid. The centroid is 380 mm below the top fibre and 420 mm above the bottom fibre. How far below the centroid is the lower kern point?

A. 175.4 mm    B. 158.7 mm    C. 258.2 mm    D. 334.2 mm

## Solution
$r^2 = \dfrac{I}{A} = \dfrac{1.2 \times 10^{10}}{180\,000} = 66\,667$ mm².
---
A force at the lower kern makes the top fibre stress zero, so $k_b = \dfrac{r^2}{c_t}$.
---
$k_b = \dfrac{66\,667}{380} = 175.4$ mm.
