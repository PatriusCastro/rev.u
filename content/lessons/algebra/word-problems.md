## Read, name, write
Name each unknown with a letter and its unit. Turn every sentence into one equation. You need as many equations as unknowns.

## Variation
- Direct: $y = kx$. Inverse: $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$. Joint: $y = kxz$.
- Find $k$ from the first case, or divide the two cases so $k$ cancels.

## Uniform motion
$$d = vt$$

- Wind or current: speed with it is $v + w$, against it is $v - w$.
- Catching up: the gap closes at $v_1 - v_2$. Moving toward each other: $v_1 + v_2$.
- Column of troops of length $L$: rear to front takes $\dfrac{L}{v_m - v_c}$, front to rear takes $\dfrac{L}{v_m + v_c}$.

## Work and mixtures
- If a job takes $a$ days alone, the rate is $\dfrac{1}{a}$ job per day. Rates working together add.
- Mixtures: track the amount of the pure substance. Amount = volume × concentration, before and after.

## Progressions
- Arithmetic: $a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d$, $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)$.
- Geometric: $a_n = a_1 r^{n-1}$, $S_n = \dfrac{a_1(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}$.
- Infinite geometric, $|r| < 1$: $S = \dfrac{a_1}{1 - r}$.

## Digits, percent and roots
- A two-digit number with tens digit $t$ and units digit $u$ is $10t + u$.
- A price after a 12% tax is $1.12P$. To go back, divide by 1.12; do not subtract 12%.
- Roots $r_1, r_2, \dots$ give the factors $(x - r_1)(x - r_2)\cdots$. Multiply by the leading coefficient.

## Worked example
A boat goes 36 km downstream in 2 h and returns upstream in 3 h. Find the speed of the current.

- Downstream: $b + c = \dfrac{36}{2} = 18$ km/h.
- Upstream: $b - c = \dfrac{36}{3} = 12$ km/h.
- Subtract: $2c = 6$, so $c = 3$ km/h.

## Common slips
- Averaging the two speeds of a round trip. Average speed is total distance over total time.
- Adding times instead of rates in work problems.
- Taking a percent off a total that already includes it. ₱1,120 with 12% VAT is ₱1,000 before tax, not ₱985.60.
- Counting a bouncing ball's first drop twice. Only the rebounds go up and down.
- Using $n$ in place of $n - 1$ in the $n$th term formula.
